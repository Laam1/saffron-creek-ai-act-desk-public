# Saffron Creek AI Act Desk

## What this desk is for
Assessing the AI systems of Saffron Creek Bank, a made-up bank licensed in Dubai's DIFC, against the EU AI Act and DIFC Data Protection Regulation 10. This is a learning project, not legal advice.

## Folders
- `bank/`: facts about Saffron Creek Bank and the AI systems it uses.
- `data/`: sample data about those systems, all made up.
- `regulations/`: the legal texts we rely on, listed in `regulations/sources.csv` with a source_id for each.
- `assessments/`: one assessment per AI system, checked against the EU AI Act and DIFC Regulation 10.
- `briefings/`: short plain-English summaries of the assessments for the bank's managers.
- `notes/`: working notes, such as which tools we chose and why.

## Rules
1. Write in plain English. Short sentences. Explain any legal term the first time you use it.
2. Never name real banks, real companies or real people.
3. Every legal claim cites an article and a source_id from regulations/sources.csv.
4. If you don't know something, write "unknown". Never guess a date, an article or a number.
5. Never send email to real people.
6. Ask me before deleting or overwriting any file.
