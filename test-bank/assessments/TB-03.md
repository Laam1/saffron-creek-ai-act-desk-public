## Why

Source check: on 8 November 2026 the Article 2 quote below was compared word for word with the official EUR-Lex text of S-01, and it matched. S-02 does not change that paragraph (Art. 1 points 2 and 3, S-02). The other source texts were not opened. See "What's missing".

**Step 1: Scope. The output is recorded as not used in the EU, so the assessment stops here.**
- The register gives the UAE as the only place the output is used. The UAE is outside the EU.
- The bank is licensed in the DIFC, according to `bank/profile.md`. The DIFC is outside the EU.
- For a provider or deployer outside the EU, the AI Act reaches "providers and deployers of AI systems that have their place of establishment or are located in a third country, where the output produced by the AI system is used in the Union" (Art. 2(1)(c), S-01). The register shows no use in the Union, so this route is not met (Art. 2(1)(c), S-01).
- The tier is therefore out of scope (Art. 2, S-01). This describes whether the AI Act applies. It says nothing about whether the system is low risk, and it does not cover DIFC law.
- The result depends on the register being right and complete. See "What's missing".

**Steps 2 to 5: Not run.**

- Annex III: not run (Annex III, S-01).
- Article 50: not run (Art. 50, S-01).
- Minimal: not considered.
