# Saffron Creek Bank: profile

This bank is made up. This is a learning project, not legal advice.

## Who we are
- Saffron Creek Bank is licensed in the DIFC (Dubai International Financial Centre). The DIFC is a financial free zone in Dubai with its own laws.
- Name of the licensing regulator: unknown (not stated in the interview).
- We sell personal loans and current accounts.
- Our customers are in the UAE and in Germany.

## Where our AI outputs are used
An "AI output" is a result an AI system produces, such as a loan decision.

| AI output | UAE | Germany |
|---|---|---|
| Loan decisions | Used | Used |
| Other AI outputs | unknown | unknown |

- Loan decisions made with AI are used for German customers.
- Outputs used in Germany can bring a system under the EU AI Act (S-01), even though the bank is in Dubai.
- **To be confirmed in step 5 with Article 2 (S-01).** Article 2 sets out who the Act applies to.
