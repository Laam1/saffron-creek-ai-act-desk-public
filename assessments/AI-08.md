# AI-08: Developer Coding Assistant

## Risk tier
needs lawyer

## Why
- The first step is scope. Scope means whether the law applies to the system at all.
- The EU AI Act covers providers (makers) and deployers (users) based outside the EU "where the output produced by the AI system is used in the Union" (Art. 2(1)(c), S-01). An "output" is a result the system produces, here suggested code.
- The register says where this output is used is unknown. So we cannot tell whether the Act applies.
- The desk rules say to stop here. The checks against Article 5, Annex III and Article 50 were not run.

## Deadline
unknown. The deadline depends on the tier, and the tier cannot be set until scope is known (Art. 2(1)(c), S-01).

## DIFC notes
DIFC Regulation 10 covers personal data processed by AI (Reg. 10.1.1(a), S-03). The EU question above does not settle this section.
- A "System" under Regulation 10 is one that can "Process Personal Data" (Reg. 10.1.1(a)(i), S-03).
- The DIFC Data Protection Law also applies only to the "Processing of Personal Data" (Art. 6(2), S-04).
- The register says AI-08 uses no personal data. On that fact, neither Regulation 10 (Reg. 10.1.1(a)(i), S-03) nor the DIFC Data Protection Law (Art. 6(2), S-04) appears to be triggered.
- We have not tested that fact. See "What's missing".

## What's missing
- **Where the output is used: unknown.** The Head of Technology needs to confirm whether any developers work in the EU, or whether the suggested code is used in the EU. `bank/profile.md` lists other AI outputs as unknown and does not say where the developers are. Once this is confirmed, repeat the assessment from step 1.
- Whether code, prompts or test data sent to AI-08 ever contain personal data: unknown. If they do, Regulation 10 (Reg. 10.1.1(a)(i), S-03), the DIFC Data Protection Law (Art. 6(2), S-04) and EU data protection law (Art. 2(1), S-05) need a fresh look.
- Whether S-04 is the latest version of the DIFC law: unknown. It is dated July 2025, and the DIFC status pages could not be opened.

## Confidence
